What these numbers and colors mean
Readiness
Oura's 0–100 morning score from HRV, resting heart rate, temperature, sleep and recent activity. Green from —, red under —.
Sleep
Green at — h or more, a common target in marathon training. Red under — h.
HRV and lowest HR
Last night judged against your own average over the past week, never against other people. HRV within 10% below it, or heart rate up to 3 bpm above it, shows amber.
Oura data
Comes from Freddy, which serves the last 7 days. A daily scheduled task saves each day into your history file, so charts and week-to-week comparisons grow as days add up.
Sleep score
Oura's 0–100 score for the night, from duration, efficiency, deep and REM sleep, and timing. Same colors as readiness.
Relative effort
Strava's heart-rate based score for how hard a workout was. Easy runs usually land around 10–30 and hard sessions 70 or more.
VO₂ max
Oura's estimate of your aerobic fitness in ml/kg/min, updated every few days. Green while it holds or rises against the estimate a week earlier, amber for a dip of up to 2, red for more.
Countdown
Counted to the end date of your current Strava training focus. When you set a new goal race as your training focus in Strava, the countdown switches to it.
